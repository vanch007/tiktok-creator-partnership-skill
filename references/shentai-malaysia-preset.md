## Interested creator flow

After the creator explicitly shows interest:

1. verify the creator handle, SHENTAI Malaysia shop, product 8015, active commission, sample availability, and September reward validity;
2. create the TikTok Target Collaboration invite only when the user authorized the action;
3. after the platform confirms the invite was sent, notify the creator to accept it and complete the free-sample application in TikTok;
4. do not ask for size, color, phone, or address in WhatsApp—the creator selects the available variant and enters delivery details in the TikTok application;
5. after the sample request is approved, notify the warehouse and wait for tracking before saying the sample has shipped.

Malay operational message after verified invite send:

```text

Boleh buka TikTok Shop Creator Center, terima jemputan itu dan lengkapkan permohonan sampel percuma di sana. Use the first sentence only after the platform confirms the invitation was sent. This is an operational shape; shorten and match the creator's observed language. If the creator has not yet accepted the collaboration, do not imply that a sample is approved.

Malay update after verified sample approval and warehouse notification:

```text

Use this only when both approval and warehouse-notification evidence exist. Do not promise a dispatch date unless the warehouse or platform confirms it.
