# Human voice for creator messages

Use this reference for every creator-facing draft or rewrite. Preserve verified facts, commercial terms, the creator's actual words, and the sender's real judgment. Remove the structures that make a message feel generated or mass-produced.

## Keep four inputs

- **Fact:** the verified product, offer, deadline, or answer the creator needs.
- **Observation:** one precise detail from the creator's real content or reply.
- **Reason:** why that detail connects to this product, format, or next step.
- **Action:** one low-effort decision or response.

If the observation or reason is missing, return `research_creator`. Do not fill the gap with praise.

## Delete template residue

Remove:

- generic praise such as “your content is a great fit,” “sangat sesuai,” or “konten Anda sangat cocok”;
- vague enthusiasm such as “exciting opportunity,” “would love to explore,” “kami sangat berharap,” or “berminat untuk explore?”;
- a brochure list of sample, commission, paid collaboration, ad budget, bestseller claims, and every product benefit in one opener;
- repeated brand introductions after the conversation is already active;
- warm-up sentences that delay the answer to a fee, sample, link, timing, or rights question;
- polished summaries that repeat the previous sentence;
- slang, emojis, honorifics, intimacy, or code-switching added only to simulate friendliness.

Do not replace one banned phrase with another stock phrase. Rewrite from the concrete fact or observed detail.

## Write the chat message

For an inbound reply, answer the creator's question in the first sentence. Add only the context needed for the next decision.

After explicit interest, operational clarity outranks re-personalizing the pitch. If a verified Target Collaboration invite was sent, name the shop/product once and ask the creator to complete the invite and free-sample application in TikTok. Do not repeat the original praise or ask for size, color, phone, or address in chat.

For first outreach, identify the sender once, mention one observed detail, state one approved offer or product reason, and ask one small question. Use the shortest version that keeps those facts; it should normally fit in one compact chat bubble.

For fees, deliverables, usage rights, or ad authorization, clarity outranks brevity. State the exact verified scope once the conversation reaches that decision; do not make legal or commercial terms vague just to sound casual.

Vary sentence order and length according to the creator's observed writing. Mirror only signals that are actually present. A formal creator does not need forced slang; an informal creator does not need copied emojis.

Each sentence must earn its place. If the same sentence could go to fifty creators after changing only the name, delete it or research a better observation.

## Translation and localization

- Preserve product names, numbers, dates, rights, and platform terms exactly after verification.
- Translate meaning, not Chinese or English sentence order.
- Use locally ordinary platform words only when the creator or country pack supports them.
- Keep mild conversational roughness when it matches the sender and does not create ambiguity.
- Never use nationality as evidence for warmth, directness, humor, hierarchy, or negotiation style.

Common phrases that need rewriting:

```text
BM: Gaya content anda nampak sesuai. / Kami sangat berharap dapat bekerjasama. / Berminat untuk explore?
EN: Your content style is a great fit. / We'd love to explore a collaboration opportunity.
ID: Konten Anda sangat cocok dengan produk kami. / Kami sangat berharap bisa bekerja sama.
```

## Shape, not script

Generic version:

```text
Hi [name], your content is a great fit for our product. We offer a free sample, commission, paid collaboration, and ad support. Interested to explore?
```

Rewrite from evidence:

```text
Hi [name]—[sender] from [brand] here. I watched your [specific content], especially [observed detail]. We're looking for a creator to show [use case] for [product], with [one approved offer]. Want me to send the product card here?
```

Do not copy the second version word for word. Adapt it to the creator's actual language, current conversation state, and verified facts.

## Final scan

- Does the first sentence answer the latest question?
- Is the observation concrete enough to verify?
- Is there only one main offer and one next step?
- Did any unverified number, claim, budget, or right slip into the message?
- Can any sentence be removed without losing useful meaning?
- Would the creator recognize why this message was sent to them specifically?
